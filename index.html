Betulog

A field and lab log for birch health surveys: score crowns in the ICP Forests defoliation classes, record stem and root symptoms, map affected trees, and tie each tree to the fungal isolates taken from it. Built for sorting out why trees in a stand decline, one tree and one isolate at a time.

Sampling prompts

    These are prompts for what to sample, drawn from general forest-pathology knowledge, not diagnoses. Check the literature and confirm every identification by culture and DNA sequencing.

    Classes follow the ICP Forests defoliation scale: 0 none (0-10%), 1 slight (11-25%), 2 moderate (26-60%), 3 severe (61-99%), 4 dead. Scoring is visual and observer dependent, so calibrate observers against each other and note the assessment date relative to leaf flush. Data stays in this browser; export CSV regularly.
