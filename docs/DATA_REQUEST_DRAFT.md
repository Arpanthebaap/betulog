**Subject:** Research Data Request: Crown Condition Assessment Data for Birch (*Betula* spp.) in Sweden (Level I & II)

Dear ICP Forests Programme Co-ordinating Centre and Swedish NFC Team,

I am writing to inquire about the data access terms and request procedure for Level I and Level II crown condition survey data for Sweden, specifically regarding silver birch (*Betula pendula*) and downy birch (*Betula pubescens*).

### 1. Research Context & Purpose
I am developing an open-source research methodology and field tool (**Betulog**, https://github.com/Arpanthebaap/betulog) aimed at improving study design, observer calibration, and the integration of symptom scoring and fungal isolation records in birch crown health surveys.

A key challenge in designing local surveys is obtaining realistic empirical estimates of:
1. Within-plot vs. between-plot variance components in birch defoliation percentages.
2. Temporal trajectory of crown transparency across recent survey years.
3. Observed baseline frequency of abiotic and biotic damaging agents recorded in birch stands across Swedish biomes.

Access to historical Swedish survey data will allow us to validate design-power simulations against real-world variance distributions, establish reference baselines for observer agreement thresholds, and assist Swedish forest researchers in planning appropriately powered field studies.

### 2. Specific Data Requested
- **Geographic Scope:** Sweden (national Level I grid plots and relevant Level II intensive monitoring plots).
- **Taxa:** *Betula pendula*, *Betula pubescens*, and *Betula* spp.
- **Variables:**
  - Plot metadata (plot ID, approximate coordinates / regional grid cell, stand age, stand type).
  - Tree-level annual crown condition assessments (defoliation %, discolouration %, ICP Forests crown class).
  - Recorded damaging agent codes (Part IV damage cause codes: stem lesions, defoliators, rot, abiotic damage).
  - Assessment year and date (to evaluate phenological/seasonal timing).
- **Time Period:** Most recent 10–15 survey years (e.g., 2010–present, or available validated series).

### 3. Data Handling, Privacy & Terms of Use
I will strictly adhere to the ICP Forests Data Policy:
- Data will be used exclusively for non-commercial academic research.
- Plot coordinates will be handled with confidentiality; no precise private location details will be disclosed.
- Proper citation of the ICP Forests Programme, the UNECE Convention on Long-range Transboundary Air Pollution (CLRTAP), and the contributing Swedish National Focal Centre will be included in any publication, pre-print, or technical report.
- We welcome collaboration, co-authorship, or advisory participation from Swedish NFC pathologists and data curators.

Could you please provide guidance on the formal application process, standard data request form, and any specific requirements from the Swedish NFC?

Thank you very much for your time and guidance.
